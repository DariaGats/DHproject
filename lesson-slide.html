“By Courier”: a 50-minute lesson

Students read O. Henry’s story in two halves, predict the ending, and then test it as a twist.

Level B2 · 50 minutes · pairs, then whole class · The Four Million (1906)

1. Warm-up and key words

8 minutes

In pairs, students talk about being angry with someone without hearing their side of the story, and then match four key words.

2. Read and predict

15 minutes

Students read part 1 and write a prediction with a clue from the text. Then they read part 2.

3. Comprehension

6 minutes

True or false, short answers, and the meaning of the slang.

4. Testing the ending

19 minutes

In pairs, students test the ending with three questions and discuss why O. Henry uses a messenger.
