Limits

What the numbers cannot say

A twist is a matter of meaning, not of form alone. The measurements can show whether the endings differ from the rest of each story in length, sentence length and voice, but they cannot show that a reader is surprised.

The twist may also come before the last paragraph, so the final passage does not always contain it. In “The Gift of the Magi,” for example, the twist is the discovery that Della has sold her hair to buy a chain for Jim’s watch, while Jim has sold his watch to buy combs for her hair. This discovery comes before the last paragraph, which is the narrator’s comment on the couple, so the measurements of the final passage would describe the comment and not the twist.

If the endings do not differ measurably, this is also a finding: the surprise could not be reduced to form.

One author, one collection

The results concern one author and one collection of 25 stories. They cannot be generalised to other writers, to other works by O. Henry or to the short story as a genre.

Some stories may have no twist. They stay in the corpus and are reported, because a counterexample is part of the result.

The tools

The sentence detection of Voyant Tools depends on punctuation. Dialogue, dashes and abbreviations can split a sentence in the wrong place, so a sample of the counts is checked by hand.

Choices of the researcher

The definition of “final passage” is a choice. It is tested with a second measurement, the last whole paragraphs of each story that together reach at least 100 words.

The coding of voice is an interpretation, which is why each ending is coded twice.
