“By Courier”: Handout 2, after the ending

A. One more expression

  • a white sport (old-fashioned)

B. Understanding the ending

  1. What does the letter say?
  2. How does the woman feel when she has read the letter? How do you know?

C. Testing the ending

Answer each question with your partner. Write one piece of evidence from the text.

  1. Reversal of expectation. Does the ending go against what you expected after the first part?
  2. Seeing it differently. After the ending, do you understand an earlier scene differently? Which one?
  3. Hidden information. Did the writer keep something from you until the end? What?

My decision: this ending is / is not a twist, because ____________.

D. Talk about it

Choose two questions and discuss them with your partner. Start with question 2.

  1. Was the woman right to judge the man without asking him? Give two reasons.
  2. Why does O. Henry tell the story through a messenger? What would change if the two people simply talked?
  3. Compare the way the woman speaks with the way the boy speaks. Find one phrase from each. What is funny or interesting when the boy repeats her words?
  4. Imagine this story today, with mobile phones. Would it still work? Would it still have a twist?
  5. The three tests all said “yes” for this story. Could a story pass one test and fail another? Invent a short example. What does this tell you about the tests?
