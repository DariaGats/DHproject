“By Courier”: Handout 1, before the ending

A. Key words

Match each word with its meaning. Write the letter.

1. pensive   2. condemn   3. loyalty   4. hearsay

  1. staying faithful to a person or a promise
  2. things you have heard from others but have not checked
  3. thinking quietly, often in a sad way
  4. to decide that someone is guilty and refuse to accept them

B. Understanding part 1

True or false? Correct the false sentences.

  1. The young man is going to Chicago.
  2. The woman has told him not to speak or write to her.
  3. The boy knows both people well.
  4. The young man gives the boy a letter for the woman.
  5. The woman sends the boy back with a message.

Answer in one or two sentences.

  1. What did the woman see in the conservatory?
  2. Why did she not ask the man to explain?
  3. Choose one expression from the slang box. Explain what it means in your own words and show which part of the text helped you.
